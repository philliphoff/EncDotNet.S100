Plan a route, set vessel and departure, see where it's tight and when it meets your settings.
First pass at the 1100×700 default window. Narrow windows are deferred to a whole-app form-factor review. The model is live: drag the departure in 1b and every screen recomputes (UKC = charted depth + water level − draft − squat). Hover the profile to move the marker on the map.
Recommendations
Profile lives in the bottom dock as a Clearance tab beside Timeline; they share the time axis and departure. Alternative: a sheet docked to the map's lower edge when the bottom dock is closed.
Headline = verdict card + window band. The card answers "this departure?", the band under the profile answers "which departures?".
Checking is a mode. Edit and Check passage are toggles that stay in the same places in the panel footer, so you turn a mode off where you turned it on. They can both be on at once. Colouring only while checking; Object information folds to an edge tab; other layers get a wash. Edits recompute live; while computing, the route goes grey-dashed.
Departure is a route value. "Follow passage" pins view time to the ETA under the cursor, so S-104/S-111 show what the ship meets (1c).
Casual first, S-421 capable. A route needs only waypoints; Passage fields are optional and unlock features (draft → Check passage, speed → ETAs). Squat is an optional fixed allowance. SOG is the entered speed; S-111 is shown, not applied to ETAs.
Words. Meets your settings · Tight (within 0,5 m) · Below your UKC setting · Low confidence (S-101 depth) · No data. Never "Safe". "Not for navigation" sits in the verdict footer only.
