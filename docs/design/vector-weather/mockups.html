Tropical cyclones, fronts and pressure centres

Mock-ups for docs/design/vector-weather.md. Decision numbers (V1–V38) refer to that note. The storm, coastline and route are made up; nothing here is real data or for navigation.

Switches the chart palette of frames 1–5. Frame 6 shows all three.
Frame 1

A hurricane over a wave forecast, at Live

Cone tinted (V3), days 4–5 stippled; radii at the view time only (V12) with dashed 34, thin 50 and heavy 64 kt outlines (V4); the storm at view time is between advisory points, so its ring is dashed and its label says est. (V11). The CAP Hurricane Warning keeps its own hatching above the cone (V37). Pick card V23, Layer stack glyph legend V25, switches V26.

Frame 2

The storm through the Timeline

The whole forecast always shows; only the storm marker and its radii follow the view time (V11). On the past track it's the best-track position (solid ring); between forecast points it's an estimate (dashed ring); beyond the last point there's no marker and the Datasets row says why. The lane marks the forecast points as steps, the issue time (filled diamond) and when the next advisory is due (hollow diamond) (V14).

Frame 3

Surface analysis over GFS isobars and barbs

Monochrome front ink, shape-coded, twice the isobars' width (V17, V18). Pips every 9 mm in screen space (V20); the stationary front alternates sides. H/L with an × at the centre and the pressure underlined (V19); the low's forecast position is dashed. Troughs drop out below 1:40M (V22).

Frame 4

Stale data

A stale advisory keeps its full weight and says its age on the chart (V29). A stale analysis fades to half opacity, because an old front is in the wrong place (V30). Both get the Stale tag and Load latest, as weather runs do today.

Frame 5

A planned route near the storm (#1093)

Finding sleeves on the legs inside the 34 kt radius at ETA; a ghost storm (dashed ring) where it is at the closest approach, with a dashed CPA line (V38). Findings are grouped as Route checks group them today.

Frame 6

Symbol sheet at Day, Dusk and Night

Classification symbols for each hemisphere (V5, V6; southern ones mirrored, the arm direction to be confirmed against the WMO glyph), radii and cone samples, front types and pressure centres. Night inks stay under luminance 0.2.

Design only. No production code. Colours: WXTC* and WXFRONT tokens proposed in §3.2 and §5.2 of the note; base chart colours approximate S-101 Day, Dusk and Night.
