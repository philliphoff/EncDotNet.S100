SoundCharts · design round 1, revision 2 · #1232 / #1233

Tropical cyclones, fronts and pressure centres

Mock-ups for docs/design/vector-weather.md. The chart shows only what's drawn on the map. Everything else is shown in the SoundCharts panel it lives in, laid out as the viewer lays it out (from MainWindow.axaml and the panel views). Pink numbers match each panel in the whole-window frames to its full-size callout. The storm, coastline and route are made up.

Chart palette and app theme together, as SoundCharts pairs them: Day with Light, Dusk with S100Dusk, Night with S100Night.
Frame 1

A hurricane at Live: the whole window

Layer stack open in the left dock (1), a pick on the storm in Object information (2), the Timeline in the bottom dock (3). On the map (4): only the chart drawing, plus the existing map overlays (scale bar, tools, compass and the Live stamp that shows while the Timeline dock is open). Decisions: V1–V12, V23, V25.

Callouts

The panels at full size

1 Layer stack: a storm draws on two S-98 planes, so it has two entries. Its symbol legend sits under the track entry and its area legend under the cone-and-radii entry, as the wave fill's legend sits under its entry today. Legend rows can show a symbol or line sample as well as a colour swatch. 2 Object information: the storm is a hit in the list, then the existing notice line, credit, identity block and attributes table; the radii are attribute rows, not a new table. 5 Display controls and 6 Library are other tabs of the same left dock.

Frame 2

The storm through the Timeline

Map only, with the Time HUD as it sits on the map while the Timeline dock is closed. The forecast always shows; the storm marker and its radii follow the view time (V11). Beyond the forecast, the Datasets row and inspector say why there's no marker (7).

Frame 3

Surface analysis: the whole window

Timeline dock closed, so the Time HUD is on the map. The Layer stack lists the analysis on Other chart overlays above the isobars, with its front-type legend (V17–V22, V25). Object information shows a cold-front pick (V24).

Frame 4

Stale data

On the map, the storm label carries the age and the analysis fades (V29, V30). Everywhere else the age shows in the panels that already show freshness: a Stale tag in Datasets, a warning line in Object information under the notice, and the Library's existing Stale tag, stale-run warning and Load latest run.

Frame 5

A planned route near the storm (#1093): the whole window

The Routes panel (8) shows the check summary card and Findings as today, with a Weather row and weather findings. Check mode folds Object information to its edge tab. The route-check legend card on the map (9) is the existing RouteCheckLegendView, with a Weather row added (V38).

Frame 6

Symbol sheet at Day, Dusk and Night

Classification symbols for each hemisphere (V5, V6; southern ones mirrored, arm direction to be confirmed against the WMO glyph), radii and cone samples, front types and pressure centres. Night inks stay under luminance 0.2.

Design only. No production code. Chart colours: WXTC* and WXFRONT tokens proposed in §3.2 and §5.2 of the note; base chart colours approximate S-101 Day, Dusk and Night. App chrome follows the viewer's XAML; pixel sizes are its own.
